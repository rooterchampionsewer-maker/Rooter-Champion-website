Financing FAQ

FINANCING QUESTIONS

Who provides the financing?+

Financing is provided by Synchrony Bank, not Rooter Champion. All applications are subject to credit approval by Synchrony Bank.

Is it really no interest for 18 months?+

No interest is charged on the promotional balance if you pay it off in full within 18 months. If you don’t, interest is charged from the purchase date. Monthly payments are required during the promotion.

What work can I finance?+

Qualifying jobs such as sewer line repair and replacement, trenchless lining, pipe bursting, hydro jetting, drain installation and water heaters. Ask us when you get your estimate.

How do I apply?+

Tap “Apply Now” on this page or ask your technician on site. You’ll get a decision from Synchrony Bank, and then we schedule your job.

Will I still get an upfront price?+

Yes. We give you an upfront price before we start, so you know exactly what you’re financing.

What are your hours?+

We are open 7 days a week: Mon–Fri 8am–10pm, Sat–Sun 8am–5pm.
