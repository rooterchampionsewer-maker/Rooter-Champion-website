Common drain and sewer problems in Whittier

Whittier has plenty of mature trees and older homes, and that combination shows up in the sewer line. The most common causes we find are tree roots, old cast iron pipe that has scaled shut, and broken sewer pipe. Early warning signs are gurgling drains or a bubbling toilet and clogs that return every year.

If sewage is coming up right now, stop using water and call. Read what to do during a sewage backup.
