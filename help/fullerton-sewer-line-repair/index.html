What usually breaks a Fullerton sewer line

Tree roots get into pipe joints and cause backups that keep coming back. Old cast iron corrodes and narrows from the inside. Broken pipe lets sewage and odor into the soil. Backups that keep returning, gurgling drains, or a sewer smell in the yard are common warning signs.

How we fix it

  • Camera inspection: find the problem and see it on video.
  • Robotic root cutting: clear roots from inside the pipe.
  • Hydro jetting: scour grease, roots and buildup off the pipe wall.
  • Trenchless lining: renew cracked or failing pipe without trenching your yard, when the line qualifies.
  • Pipe bursting: when a line is too damaged to line, we replace it without digging. See how it works on our trenchless sewer repair page.
  • Sewer repair: fix broken sections that lining can't.
