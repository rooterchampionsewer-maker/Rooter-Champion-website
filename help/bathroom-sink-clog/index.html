Slow or Clogged Bathroom Sink in La Mirada

A bathroom sink that drains slowly or holds water is one of the most common clogs we see. The good news: it is usually close to the surface, and you can often fix it yourself in a few minutes.

What you might notice

  • Water drains slowly or stands in the bowl after you brush your teeth or shave
  • A musty smell coming from the drain
  • Water rises when you run the faucet for more than a few seconds
  • The pop-up stopper is stuck or barely moves

Likely causes

  • Hair and soap scum wrapped around the pop-up stopper
  • Toothpaste, shaving cream and skin-care products coating the pipe
  • A build-up in the P-trap, the U-shaped pipe under the sink
  • Less often: a clog further down the branch line inside the wall

Safe things to try first

  1. Pull out the pop-up stopper (most lift out or unscrew) and clean the hair and gunk off it.
  2. Use a cheap plastic hair-snake tool to pull hair out of the drain opening.
  3. Plug the overflow hole near the top of the sink with a wet rag, then use a small cup plunger with a few inches of water in the bowl.
  4. Flush with hot tap water for a minute. Use hot tap water, not boiling water.
  5. If you are handy, put a bucket under the sink and remove the P-trap to clean it out. Hand-tighten it when you put it back.
Why we don’t recommend chemical drain cleaners: Skip the chemical drain cleaners. They are strong caustics or acids that can burn skin and eyes, can damage older pipes and fittings, and often only eat a small hole through the clog. When they do not work, the drain is left full of chemical water that can splash you or whoever opens the line next. Never mix drain products, because some combinations give off toxic fumes.

If water starts rising or you see sewage, stop and call. Don’t take apart anything you’re not comfortable putting back together.

When to call a pro

  • The sink is still slow after you cleaned the stopper and trap
  • More than one drain in the house is slow, or the tub gurgles when the sink drains
  • The clog comes back every few weeks
  • You see water leaking under the sink or inside the cabinet

Common questions

Why does my bathroom sink keep clogging?

Hair and soap scum build up a little every day. If the stopper and P-trap are not cleaned now and then, the build-up narrows the pipe until water can barely get through. A drain that clogs again within a few weeks of cleaning may have a deeper build-up in the wall line.

Is boiling water safe for a bathroom sink?

We do not recommend it. Boiling water can soften PVC pipe joints and can crack porcelain. Hot tap water is safer and works just as well for flushing soap residue.
