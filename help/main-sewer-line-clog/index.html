Several Drains Backing Up: Main Sewer Line Clog in La Habra

When one sink is slow, the problem is usually that sink. When several drains act up at once, the problem is usually the main sewer line that carries everything from your home to the street. That needs attention soon, before it turns into a backup.

What you might notice

  • More than one fixture is slow or clogged at the same time
  • Water comes up in the tub or shower when you flush the toilet
  • The lowest drain in the house, often a tub or floor drain, backs up first
  • Gurgling in toilets and drains when the washer or dishwasher drains
  • Water or sewage around the outdoor cleanout cap

Likely causes

  • Tree roots growing into the line through joints or cracks
  • Wipes, paper towels and grease that collect in the main line
  • A sagging section of pipe (a "belly") where waste settles
  • A cracked, offset or collapsed section of older clay or cast iron pipe

Safe things to try first

  1. Stop using water: no laundry, dishwasher, showers or flushing until the line is cleared.
  2. Tell everyone in the house so nobody runs water by accident.
  3. If you know where your outdoor cleanout is, look at it but do not open the cap yourself. It can be under pressure from backed-up sewage.
  4. If sewage is already coming up inside, see our sewage backup guide and call right away.
Why we don’t recommend chemical drain cleaners: Skip the chemical drain cleaners. They are strong caustics or acids that can burn skin and eyes, can damage older pipes and fittings, and often only eat a small hole through the clog. When they do not work, the drain is left full of chemical water that can splash you or whoever opens the line next. Never mix drain products, because some combinations give off toxic fumes.

If water starts rising or you see sewage, stop and call. Don’t take apart anything you’re not comfortable putting back together.

When to call a pro

  • Right away. A main line clog will not clear itself, and the next load of laundry or shower can push sewage up into the house.
  • Especially if water is rising in a tub, shower or floor drain

Common questions

How do I know if it is the main line or just one drain?

If only one fixture is slow, it is usually that drain. If several are slow, or water appears in a tub or shower when you flush, it is usually the main line.

Can I keep using one bathroom until the plumber comes?

It is best not to. Any water you use has to go through the same blocked main line, and it can come up in the lowest drain.

Why does my main line keep clogging every year?

Repeat clogs often mean tree roots or a damaged section of pipe. A camera inspection shows which one it is so you can decide on a longer-term fix.
