Broken or Old Sewer Pipe and Trenchless Repair in La Mirada

Many homes in our area still have their original sewer pipe. Over the years, clay and cast iron can crack, shift at the joints, sag or rust. The first step is always seeing the pipe from the inside with a camera, so you know exactly what you are dealing with.

What you might notice

  • Main line clogs that keep coming back
  • A sunken or soggy area in the yard over the sewer line
  • Sewer smell outside or under the house
  • A home inspector noted a possible sewer problem
  • The house is older and the sewer line has never been inspected

Likely causes

  • Cracked or broken clay pipe
  • Cast iron that has rusted, scaled or cracked over time
  • Joints that have shifted apart (offsets) and let roots in
  • A sagging section ("belly") where waste and water collect
  • Ground movement or heavy loads over the pipe

Safe things to try first

  1. There is no safe do-it-yourself repair for a broken sewer pipe.
  2. Limit water use if drains are slow, and avoid flushing wipes or grease.
  3. Keep notes on when clogs happen. That history helps us plan.
  4. If you are buying a home, ask for a sewer camera inspection before your contingency period ends.

If water starts rising or you see sewage, stop and call. Don’t take apart anything you’re not comfortable putting back together.

When to call a pro

  • You have had more than one main line clog in a year
  • You see a soggy or sunken spot in the yard
  • You are buying or selling a home
  • A previous plumber told you the line is damaged and you want a second opinion

Common questions

What is trenchless pipe lining?

A flexible liner coated with resin is put inside the existing pipe and cured in place, creating a new pipe inside the old one. It is done through access points, so you avoid digging up the whole yard.

Should I get a sewer inspection before buying a home?

It is a good idea, especially for older homes or homes with big trees. A standard home inspection usually does not include a camera look inside the sewer line.

Can I get a second opinion on a sewer repair quote?

Yes. We can run our own camera inspection and show you the footage so you can compare with what you were told.
