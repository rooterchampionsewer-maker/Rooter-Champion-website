Common Plumbing Issues in Rowland Heights

WHAT WE SEE IN ROWLAND HEIGHTS

Restaurant and kitchen grease

Rowland Heights has a busy restaurant scene. Commercial kitchen lines collect grease fast, and hydro jetting is the best way to keep them flowing.

Hillside homes

Homes on sloped lots can have long sewer runs where soil movement leads to offset joints or low spots.

Tree roots

Roots from mature landscaping can work into older pipe joints and cause repeat backups.

Yard drains

We clear yard and patio drains with hydro jetting only.

Rowland Heights FAQ

QUESTIONS FROM ROWLAND HEIGHTS CUSTOMERS

Do you serve Rowland Heights?+

Yes. We serve Rowland Heights from our shop at 1401 S Beach Blvd in La Habra.

What does the $55 drain cleaning include?+

The $55 special covers indoor and main-line drains and includes a camera inspection. Yard and storm drains are cleared with hydro jetting only, so they are quoted separately.

Do you clean restaurant drains?+

Yes. We clear and hydro jet commercial kitchen lines and can camera-inspect them to find recurring problems.

How do I know if my sewer line needs repair?+

Repeat backups, gurgling toilets or several slow drains at once usually point to the main line. A sewer camera inspection shows what is going on before you spend money on repairs.
